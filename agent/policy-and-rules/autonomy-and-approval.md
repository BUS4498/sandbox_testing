# Autonomy and Approval Policy

## Pre-authorized operations

The future agent may, within student-configured scope:

- read local context and approved posting sources;
- analyze, compare, prioritize, and recommend;
- update local opportunity and memory records;
- save drafts locally;
- send an operational notification to the student's verified address after a material update is durably recorded; and
- execute a student-enabled daily run under the same rules as Run Now.

## Explicit approval required

Approval of the exact action is required before:

- submitting or withdrawing an application;
- changing final application materials or answers;
- sending any recruiter, employer, alumni, reference, or other third-party communication;
- uploading student data or files to a new external service;
- disclosing sensitive data;
- deleting material history or bulk-overwriting student-entered records; or
- changing the scope of monitored sources beyond the student's configuration.

## Approval record

An approval must identify the student, action, target, exact content or version, affected records, time, expiration or one-time scope, and any conditions. Silence, prior similar approval, or schedule enablement is not approval.

Approval may be revoked before execution. If the target or content changes, obtain new approval. Record both approval and resulting verification outcome.

## Prohibited autonomy

Application submission, qualification fabrication, unapproved final-material edits, and unapproved third-party communications are never autonomous operations.
